FAQ & policies

The details, straight.

Is this AI music?

Yes — and no. The instrumentation and vocals come from AI tools; every song then goes through real human production — story interpretation, prompt craft, a full quality listen, and the art and video work. Think of it like film: the camera captures the image, but the director makes the movie.

Can you remix or edit a song I already love?

No — and that’s on purpose. Every song we make is brand-new, written from scratch for your moment. We don’t remix, rework, or edit existing or commercial tracks. You can absolutely tell us what you want it to sound like, and we’ll craft something original in that spirit.

Who owns the song?

You do — full commercial-use rights to play, share, and monetize it. AI-made music sits in an evolving legal space, so for high-stakes commercial use (national ads, film/TV, major-label release) we’d point you to an entertainment attorney. For everything else, it’s yours to keep forever.

How many revisions do I get?

1 with The Song, 2 with The Experience, 3 with The Legacy — free. If it’s still not right after that, more revisions are 50% off. We’d rather get it right than call it done.

Can I get a refund?

Before we start producing, yes — full refund, no questions. Once production begins, we don’t refund completed work, but the revision path is there to get you to a song you love.

How fast will I get it?

The Song in under 24 hours, The Experience in 3 business days, The Legacy in 7 — faster with Rush. Tiers with photos or voice start their clock when your files arrive.

What happens after delivery?

You’ve got 14 days to request any included revisions. After that, the order closes out automatically.
